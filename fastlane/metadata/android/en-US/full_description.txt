Skuare is a minimal, open-source companion app for Casio G-Shock watches. It speaks the watch's Bluetooth protocol directly and never sends your data over the internet: the app has no network permission at all.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Pair through the Android companion device manager</li>
<li>Automatic time adjustment in the background when the watch connects</li>
<li>Home time and world time zones, including DST, coordinates and radio signal settings</li>
<li>Alarms and hourly signal</li>
<li>Timer</li>
<li>Reminder titles and settings</li>
<li>Watch settings: name, connection timeout, time adjustment, date and time display, power saving, backlight duration and auto backlight</li>
</ul>

<b>Tested modules</b>
3459, 3461, 3539, 3540, 3575 and 3578.
