# Privacy Policy for Skuare

**Effective date:** October 20, 2026

Skuare is an independent, open-source companion app for supported Bluetooth Casio G-Shock watches. Skuare is developed and maintained by Dino Leung.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Skuare accesses, how that information is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

## Summary

Skuare is designed to communicate directly with your watch over Bluetooth. The app:

- does not require an account;
- does not include advertising or analytics SDKs;
- does not send your watch data or app data to the developer's servers;
- does not sell or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers; and
- does not request or use your device's location.

## Information Skuare accesses

To discover, pair with, and communicate with a supported watch, Skuare may access:

- nearby Bluetooth device scan results;
- the watch's Bluetooth address, advertised name, and user-configured name;
- watch settings and status communicated over Bluetooth, such as time-zone settings, alarms, reminders, timers, battery status, and synchronization status;
- the Android Companion Device Manager association for a paired watch; and
- technical error information produced while pairing, connecting, or synchronizing.

Bluetooth scan access is used only to find supported watches. Skuare declares that Bluetooth scanning is not used to derive physical location and does not request Android location permission.

## Information stored on your device

Skuare stores the following information in its local app database so that paired watches can be recognized and synchronized:

- Bluetooth address and watch names;
- the Android companion-device association identifier;
- pairing and last-synchronization times;
- the most recent synchronization error, if any; and
- a randomly generated installation identifier used to associate this installation of Skuare with your watch.

The installation identifier is generated by the app, is not based on an advertising identifier or hardware identifier, and is not used to track you across apps or services.

Skuare may also write technical Bluetooth communication and error details to the device's local system log for troubleshooting. Skuare does not transmit these logs to the developer.

## How the information is used

Skuare uses the information it accesses only to provide its watch-companion features, including:

- discovering, pairing with, and remembering supported watches;
- reading and changing watch settings;
- synchronizing the date, time, and time zones;
- managing alarms, timers, and reminders;
- detecting when a paired watch is nearby; and
- performing watch synchronization in the background and showing related notifications.

## Data transmission and sharing

Skuare does not operate a backend service and does not transmit app or watch data to the developer. Skuare does not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties for advertising, analytics, profiling, or marketing.

Pairing and background presence detection use Android's system-provided Companion Device Manager. Bluetooth communication occurs directly between your device and your watch.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may include Skuare's local app data in a device backup or device-to-device transfer. These features are controlled by you and your operating-system or backup provider. Skuare's developer does not receive or have access to those backups.

## Permissions

Skuare may request or use these Android permissions and capabilities:

- **Nearby devices / Bluetooth scan and connect:** finds and communicates with supported watches.
- **Companion-device presence and background operation:** detects a paired watch and supports synchronization while the app is not open.
- **Foreground service:** maintains an active Bluetooth synchronization session.
- **Notifications:** informs you about synchronization activity or asks you to open Skuare when watch interaction requires it.

You can deny or revoke optional permissions through Android settings. Some app features will not work without the permissions they require.

## Data retention and deletion

Local information about a paired watch is retained until you use Skuare's **Forget** action. You can delete all locally stored Skuare data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Android-managed backups may remain subject to the retention and deletion practices of your operating-system or backup provider.

Skuare does not provide user accounts and therefore holds no server-side account data to delete.

## Security

Skuare relies on Android's app sandbox, permission system, Companion Device Manager, and Bluetooth security features to protect locally stored information and watch communication. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection, but Skuare minimizes risk by avoiding developer-operated servers and unnecessary data transmission.

## Children's privacy

Skuare is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
